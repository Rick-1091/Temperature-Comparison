04

Weather × activity · Network visualization

How are weather conditions and everyday activities connected?

A bipartite network of observed co-occurrence. A link appears when an activity’s average level on days with that condition is at least 5% above its average across all days. Hover to trace connections; click to filter the other views.

ObservedInferred

Connection strength represents observed association, not causation.

Evidence · Data · Limitations

How do we know?

Every number on this page traces back to one of three sources and a small set of transformations. Here is what they are, and what they cannot tell you.

Claim boundaries

  • 01Market probability is not an official weather forecast.
  • 02Association does not imply causation.
  • 03Historical activity patterns do not guarantee future behavior.
  • 04Observed users may not represent all digital nomads.
  • 05Missing data is visibly marked rather than silently interpolated.
