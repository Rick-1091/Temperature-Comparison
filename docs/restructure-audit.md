## Scientific boundary
Market prices are not voter shares or calibrated forecast probabilities. METAR maxima need not match settlement. Nine days cannot establish skill. Fictional decisions and rainfall gameplay do not establish economic losses or real forecasts. Official warnings take priority. Hedging needs matched location/date/variable/settlement, liquidity, costs and basis risk.
